The Problem

Jharkhand's PALASH programme teaches Ho, Mundari, Santhali, Kurukh & Kharia in 1,000+ tribal schools. But most posted teachers are Hindi-medium trained, with no tools to teach in the child's mother tongue — and these languages have almost no digital NLP resources to build on.

Our Solution

Bhasha Setu is an AI co-teacher: it listens to the teacher's Hindi, translates it live into the classroom's tribal language with a visible confidence score, reads it aloud, and can turn an entire Hindi lesson PDF into a translated worksheet — all running locally, so it works in classrooms with no signal.

How It Works

1

Speak or type in Hindi

The teacher speaks into the mic (live Hindi speech recognition) or picks a ready-made classroom phrase.

2

Real-data translation engine

Matched against a real corpus first, falling back to a trained statistical lexicon — every result is labeled with an honest confidence score.

3

Spoken, displayed & downloadable

The classroom display speaks and shows the translation, and full PDFs can be translated and downloaded — all offline.
